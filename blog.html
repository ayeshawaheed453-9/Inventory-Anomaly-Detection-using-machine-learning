PROJECT NOTES

Machine Learning-Based Inventory Anomaly Detection System

A beginner-friendly guide to the business problem, unsupervised models, thresholding, and responsible action.

What problem are we solving?

Inventory teams need to decide which records deserve investigation: high stock with weak sales, low stock with strong sales, unusual orders, sharp demand changes, and uncommon combinations of variables.

Why unsupervised learning?

The Kaggle dataset does not provide a trusted fraud or anomaly label. Therefore the system learns the structure of typical observations instead of pretending a target column exists. The interface may show NORMAL or ANOMALY, but that is a thresholded model score, not supervised classification.

How the models work

  • IQR baseline: flags values far outside the interquartile range.
  • KDE: estimates density; low-density observations are more unusual.
  • GMM: models behavior as a mixture of Gaussian patterns; low likelihood is unusual.
  • PCA: compresses features into two components for visual analysis.
  • K-Means: clusters observations; distance from a center is comparative evidence, not a standalone anomaly proof.

Score and threshold

A standardized 0–1 score combines KDE, GMM, K-Means distance, and the baseline. The default threshold is the 95th percentile of training scores, so the investigation workload is explicit and configurable in the metadata.

From detection to decision

Model output is a prioritization signal. A high stock-to-sales ratio suggests reviewing overstock; low coverage suggests replenishment review; large order-to-sales ratio suggests checking the purchase plan; sharp demand change suggests validating the underlying demand signal.

Responsible interpretation: an anomaly is not automatically fraud, theft, or an incorrect record. A person must investigate context before acting.

Limitations and next steps

The Kaggle source is synthetic and may not represent a company’s exact operating behavior. Unsupervised models can generate false positives, and different products may have different normal patterns. Future work could add real company data, alerts, role-based access, database storage, and time-aware validation.
